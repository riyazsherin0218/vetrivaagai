The Process

How a Vetrivaagai chit works

A simple four-step cycle that repeats every month until all 25 members have received their chit.

1. Members Pool

25 members contribute their monthly instalment to form the chit fund.

2. Open Auction

The member offering the highest discount wins that month's chit.

3. Discount Shared

After 5% foreman commission, the discount is divided among all members.

4. Prize Paid

The winner receives the chit amount minus the discount they offered.
