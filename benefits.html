Why Chit Fund

Company Specialities

What makes a Vetrivaagai group different

Twelve things we do that members tell us they don't find elsewhere.

Pay daily, weekly or monthly

Members can split the chit instalment into daily, weekly or monthly payments to suit their own cash flow. Your income pattern, your payment pattern.

Short-term savings

A 25-month cycle is short enough to stay committed to, and long enough to accumulate capital that actually changes something.

Up to 15% for late takers

Members who take their chit at the end of the cycle earn up to 15% interest on what they have saved — simply for waiting.

Open auction, in front of everyone

The bidding process is conducted transparently in the presence of all members. No sealed envelopes, no private deals.

Fixed day, fixed time

Every auction happens on the announced date at the announced hour, every month, without exception. You can plan around it.

Foreman commission only 5%

Our commission is capped at 5% of the chit amount. There is no processing fee, service charge or hidden deduction on top of it.

Maximum discount 30%

Bidding is capped at 30% so no member is ever pushed into an unreasonable discount to access funds urgently.

Fair lottery at the cap

When several members bid the maximum 30%, the subscriber is selected by an open lottery method — not by who spoke loudest.

Simple, proper documentation

Clear agreements, proper receipts and complete member records. Everything you sign, you get a copy of.

New enrolment every month

Member enrolment happens every single month, so you never have to wait long for a group that fits your budget.

Not an RD, FD or insurance plan

Unlike recurring deposits, fixed deposits or life insurance, a chit is a short-term savings plan and an investment — with liquidity when you need it.

Not linked to the stock market

Unlike a mutual fund, your return is not tied to market movement. The rate is determined by the auction, and it is stable.
