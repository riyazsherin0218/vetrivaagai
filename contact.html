Common Questions

Before you call us

How much do I need to start?

The smallest group is TF M at ₹2,000 a month. First-month members pay an additional one-time ₹500 enrolment charge.

When is the auction held?

On a fixed announced day, at a fixed announced time, every month — in front of all members. The schedule is given to you when you join.

Can I pay weekly instead of monthly?

Yes. Members can split the instalment into daily, weekly or monthly payments to match their own cash flow.

When do I receive the chit amount?

Immediately after your surety and securities are completed and the documentation is in order.

What if two members bid the maximum 30%?

The subscriber is selected by an open lottery method, conducted in the presence of all members.

What do I earn if I never bid?

You collect a dividend every month, and the last chit takers receive up to 15% interest on their savings.
