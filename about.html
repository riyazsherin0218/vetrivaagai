9+ Years Serving Our Members

A chit is not just a savings plan — it is a promise between neighbours. Our only job is to keep that promise, month after month.

Vetrivaagai began with a simple observation: families in and around Sivagiri were saving diligently, but rarely had a safe, well-documented way to turn those savings into a lump sum when life asked for one — a wedding, a shop, a first home.

So we built the company we would want to be a member of. A fixed auction date. An open bidding floor where every member can watch. A foreman commission capped at 5%. Written agreements and clean records for every single group. Nine years on, that discipline is still the whole business.

If you are considering your first chit, come to the branch and ask us anything. We would rather you join with your questions answered than with your fingers crossed.
