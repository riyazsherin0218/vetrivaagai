Your subscription falls

Notice how the monthly subscription drops from ₹8,000 to around ₹6,000 once dividends start flowing back. You pay less as the group matures.

Dividend is shared

After the 5% foreman commission, the entire discount offered by the winning bidder is split equally among all 25 members — every single month.

Late takers earn more

Members who take their chit towards the end of the cycle receive up to 15% effective return on their savings, because they have collected dividends all along.
